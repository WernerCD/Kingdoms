## The Golden Rule

> **"Whenever a card's text directly contradicts these rules, the card takes precedence."**

This is the foundational rule of Magic. Official rules provide the framework, but individual card text always wins when there's a conflict.

---

## Game Overview

Magic: The Gathering is a trading card game where players represent powerful wizards (Planeswalkers) summoning creatures, casting spells, and using artifacts to reduce their opponents' life total to zero.

| Setting | Default Rule |
|---|---|
| Starting life | **20** (Commander: 40) |
| Opening hand | 7 cards |
| Players | 2+ |
| Win condition | Reduce all opponents to 0 life |

---

## Deck Construction

- **Minimum deck size:** 60 cards (Commander: 100)
- **No maximum** — but you must be able to shuffle without assistance
- **Card limit:** Maximum **4 copies** of any card (except basic lands)
- **Sideboard:** Up to 15 cards, swappable between games in a match
- **Basic lands:** Unlimited copies; no restriction

## Card Types

### Permanents
Cards that stay on the battlefield after being cast:

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Land** | Tap to produce mana; play one free per turn (not a spell) |
| **Creature** | Has Power/Toughness; can attack and block |
| **Enchantment** | Persistent magical effect; Auras attach to other cards |
| **Artifact** | Magical items; Equipment can be re-equipped to creatures |
| **Planeswalker** | Powerful ally with loyalty abilities; can be attacked |
| **Battle** | Newer card type; can be attacked by opponents |

### Non-Permanents
Cards that go to the graveyard after resolving:

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Sorcery** | Cast only during your main phase when the stack is empty |
| **Instant** | Cast at any time, including opponents' turns and in response to other spells |

---

## Zones

Every card in Magic exists in one of these zones at all times:

| Zone | Description |
|---|---|
| **Library** | Your face-down shuffled deck |
| **Hand** | Cards in your hand (private; max 7 at end of turn) |
| **Battlefield** | Cards currently in play |
| **Graveyard** | Discard pile; face-up, ordered |
| **Stack** | Spells and abilities that have been cast but not yet resolved |
| **Exile** | Permanent removal zone; cards usually cannot be played from here |
| **Command Zone** | Commander format only — where commanders live |

## Mana System

Mana is the resource used to cast spells. There are **6 types**:

| Color | Symbol | Basic Land |
|---|---|---|
| White | {W} | Plains |
| Blue | {U} | Island |
| Black | {B} | Swamp |
| Red | {R} | Mountain |
| Green | {G} | Forest |
| Colorless | {C} | Wastes |

**Mana costs** appear in a card's top-right corner. Generic mana (numbers) can be paid with any color. Colored symbols must be paid with that specific color.

> **Rule of thumb:** Include ~20–24 lands in a 60-card deck (~1/3 of the deck) to avoid being mana-starved or mana-flooded.

## Turn Structure

Each turn proceeds through **5 phases** in order:

---

### 1. Beginning Phase

**a) Untap Step**
- Untap all permanents you control
- No spells or abilities can be played here

**b) Upkeep Step**
- Triggered "at the beginning of upkeep" abilities resolve
- Players may cast instants and activate abilities

**c) Draw Step**
- Draw one card from your library
- The player who goes first skips this on turn one

---

### 2. First Main Phase

- Cast any spells you can afford (creatures, sorceries, instants, artifacts, enchantments, planeswalkers)
- Play **one land** (your land drop for the turn)
- Activate abilities

---

### 3. Combat Phase

Combat resolves in **5 steps**:

**a) Beginning of Combat**
- Last chance to cast instants before attackers are declared

**b) Declare Attackers**
- Choose which untapped creatures attack (and which player/planeswalker they attack)
- Attacking creatures become **tapped**

**c) Declare Blockers**
- Defending player assigns untapped creatures to block attackers
- Multiple creatures may block a single attacker
- Each blocker can only block one attacker
- Tapped creatures cannot block

**d) Combat Damage**
- Each creature deals damage equal to its **Power**
- Creatures take damage equal to their **Toughness** to be destroyed
- Unblocked attackers deal damage directly to the defending player/planeswalker
- If multiple blockers: attacking player assigns damage distribution among them

**e) End of Combat**
- Triggered "end of combat" abilities resolve
- Opportunity for instants and abilities

---

### 4. Second Main Phase

- Identical to the first main phase
- You may cast spells, but **cannot play another land** if you already played one

---

### 5. Ending Phase

**a) End Step**
- "At the beginning of the end step" triggers resolve
- Last chance to cast instants before cleanup

**b) Cleanup Step**
- Discard down to maximum hand size (7 cards by default)
- All damage is removed from creatures
- "Until end of turn" effects expire
- No player receives priority (unless a triggered ability fires)

---

## The Stack & Priority

When a spell is cast or an ability is activated, it goes on the **stack** and doesn't resolve immediately. This lets players respond.

- Spells and abilities resolve **last-in, first-out** (newest resolves first)
- Each player may respond before each resolution
- When all players **pass priority** in succession, the top item resolves
- Lands bypass the stack entirely — they enter the battlefield immediately
- **Countered spells** go to the graveyard without resolving

---

## Abilities

**Activated Abilities** — Format: `[Cost]: [Effect]`
- Activate any time you have priority (unless restricted)
- Example: `{T}: Add {G}` (tap to add green mana)

**Triggered Abilities** — Begin with "when," "whenever," or "at"
- Automatically trigger when conditions are met
- Enter the stack and can be responded to

**Static Abilities** — Written as statements
- Always in effect; don't use the stack
- Example: "Creatures you control have flying"

---

## Common Keyword Abilities (Evergreen)

These appear in virtually every set:

| Keyword | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Flying** | Can only be blocked by creatures with flying or reach |
| **Reach** | Can block creatures with flying |
| **Trample** | Excess combat damage carries over to the player/planeswalker |
| **First Strike** | Deals combat damage before creatures without first strike |
| **Double Strike** | Deals both first strike and regular combat damage |
| **Deathtouch** | Any amount of damage this deals is enough to destroy a creature |
| **Lifelink** | Damage dealt also heals the controller |
| **Vigilance** | Doesn't tap when attacking |
| **Haste** | Can attack and use tap abilities the turn it enters the battlefield |
| **Flash** | Can be cast any time you could cast an instant |
| **Hexproof** | Cannot be targeted by opponents' spells or abilities |
| **Indestructible** | Cannot be destroyed by damage or effects that say "destroy" |
| **Menace** | Must be blocked by two or more creatures |
| **Shroud** | Cannot be targeted by any spells or abilities (including yours) |

## Planeswalker Rules

- Planeswalkers are **not creatures** and cannot attack or block
- Opponents' creatures may **attack planeswalkers** instead of you
- Unblocked damage to a planeswalker removes **loyalty counters**
- Planeswalkers at **0 loyalty** die and go to the graveyard
- You may activate **one loyalty ability per turn** during your main phase
  - Positive abilities add loyalty counters
  - Negative abilities remove loyalty counters
- **Legend Rule:** If you control two planeswalkers with the same name, sacrifice all but one

---

## Losing and Winning

**A player loses the game when:**
- Their life total reaches **0**
- They must draw a card but their library is empty
- They accumulate **10 poison counters**
- A card effect says they lose the game
- They have to put more than one token into play and can't (e.g., Platinum Angel scenarios)

**A player wins the game when:**
- All opponents have lost
- A card effect says they win the game (e.g., Thassa's Oracle)

---

## Mulligan Rules (London Mulligan)

If you're unhappy with your opening hand:
1. Shuffle your entire hand back into your library
2. Draw **7 new cards**
3. Repeat as many times as desired
4. For **each mulligan taken**, put one card from your hand on the bottom of your library
5. You may keep any hand of 0 or more cards

> Example: Take 2 mulligans → draw 7, put 2 on the bottom → play with 5 cards

---

## Creature Combat — Key Rules

- **Summoning Sickness:** A creature cannot attack or use tap abilities the turn it enters the battlefield (unless it has **Haste**)
- **Tapped creatures** cannot attack or block
- **Lethal damage:** A creature is destroyed when it has taken damage equal to or greater than its toughness during a single turn
- **Damage resets** at the cleanup step each turn
- **Deathtouch** overrides toughness — any damage is lethal
- **Indestructible** creatures survive any amount of damage

## Format Categories

### Constructed
Decks built before play using cards you own:

| Format | Card Pool |
|---|---|
| **Standard** | Most recent ~2 years of sets |
| **Pioneer** | Sets from 2012 onward |
| **Modern** | Sets from 2003 onward |
| **Legacy** | All cards (some banned) |
| **Vintage** | All cards (restricted list, no bans) |
| **Commander** | All cards; 100-card singleton, multiplayer |

### Limited
Deck built from freshly opened product:

| Format | Description |
|---|---|
| **Sealed Deck** | Open 6 booster packs, build a 40-card deck |
| **Booster Draft** | Draft cards from boosters with a group, build 40-card deck |

## Quick Reference

```
TURN ORDER
─────────────────────────────────────────
1. Beginning Phase
   • Untap → Upkeep → Draw

2. First Main Phase
   • Cast spells · Play 1 land

3. Combat Phase
   • Begin Combat → Attackers → Blockers
   → Damage → End Combat

4. Second Main Phase
   • Cast spells (no more land plays)

5. Ending Phase
   • End Step → Cleanup (discard to 7, reset damage)

LIFE TOTALS
• Standard: 20 life
• Commander: 40 life

WIN / LOSS
• Win: Reduce all opponents to 0 life
• Lose: 0 life · empty library draw · 10 poison counters

THE STACK
• Instants & abilities resolve last-in, first-out
• All players must pass priority for resolution
• Lands don't use the stack
```
