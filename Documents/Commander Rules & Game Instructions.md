# Commander (EDH) – Official Rules & Game Instructions

> **Source:** [mtgcommander.net](https://mtgcommander.net/index.php/rules/) & [magic.wizards.com](https://magic.wizards.com/en/formats/commander)  
> **Last updated:** September 2026

---

## What is Commander?

Commander (also known as EDH — Elder Dragon Highlander) is a constructed multiplayer format for Magic: The Gathering. Each player builds a 100-card singleton deck led by a legendary creature called their **commander**. Games are designed for 3–5 players, are free-for-all multiplayer, and typically last around 120 minutes (~20 minutes per player).

---

## Deck Construction Rules

### Rule 1 – Commander Selection
Players designate a **legendary creature** as their deck's commander. The commander begins the game face-up in the **Command Zone**.

- Some legendary planeswalkers or non-creature cards with "can be your commander" text are also eligible, depending on group agreement.

### Rule 2 – Color Identity
All cards in the deck must belong to the **color identity** of the commander.

- A card's color identity includes its casting cost **and** any mana symbols in its rules text.
- **Hybrid mana symbols** (e.g., {W/U}) contribute **all** their colors to color identity.
- **Colorless cards** may be included in any deck.

### Rule 3 – Deck Size
Decks contain **exactly 100 cards**, including the commander.

- If using a **Companion**, it occupies a 101st card slot (outside the deck), but must still adhere to color identity restrictions.

### Rule 4 – Singleton Restriction
With the exception of basic lands, **no two cards in the deck may share the same English name**.

- Some cards override this restriction through their own card text.

### Card Legality
- Only official Wizards of the Coast **black- or white-bordered** cards are permitted.
- Silver-bordered, acorn, and gold-bordered cards require prior group approval.
- Any card in the Commander ban list (see below) may not be used.

## Starting the Game

| Setting | Rule |
|---|---|
| Starting life total | **40 life** per player |
| Commander placement | Face-up in the Command Zone |
| Turn order | Clockwise; any player may attack any opponent |
| Hand size | 7 cards (standard mulligans apply) |

---

## Play Rules

### Rule 5 – Starting Life
Each player begins with **40 life**.

### Rule 6 – Casting from the Command Zone
Your commander may be cast from the Command Zone at any time you could normally cast it, **plus an additional {2} (two generic mana) for each previous time it has been cast from the Command Zone** this game.

- This additional cost is called the **commander tax**.

### Rule 7 – Commander Zone Return
When your commander would go to the **graveyard** or **exile**, you may instead return it to the Command Zone.

- It may also return to the Command Zone instead of your hand or library (if an effect would put it there).

### Rule 8 – Commander as Permanent Property
Being a commander is **not a characteristic** (like color or type) — it is a property of the card itself. This status persists through **zone changes** and **control changes**.

### Rule 9 – Commander Damage (Poison Rule)
A player who has been dealt **21 or more combat damage by the same commander** over the course of the game **loses the game**, regardless of life total.

- This damage is tracked cumulatively across the entire game, even if the commander changes zones.

### Rule 10 – Outside-Game Cards
Abilities that fetch cards from outside the game (e.g., **Wishes**) are **non-functional** in Commander by default.

---

## Winning and Losing

- Players lose when they reach **0 life**, have taken **21+ commander damage** from a single commander, or accumulate **10 poison counters**.
- Players also lose if they cannot draw a card from an empty library.
- Standard "win the game" effects work normally.
- The last player standing wins.

---

## Multiplayer Specifics

- Players may **attack any opponent** and target any player with spells and abilities (unless restricted by card text).
- Turns proceed clockwise.
- Elimination: When a player loses, all cards they own leave the game. Any effects they controlled end.

## The Social Contract & Rule Zero

Commander is fundamentally a **social format**. The community strongly encourages a brief pre-game conversation about:

- Power level expectations
- Specific strategies or cards to avoid
- Game length preferences

**Rule Zero** allows a playgroup to modify any official rule **by unanimous group consensus**. However, Rule Zero does not allow a single player to unilaterally announce changes — all players must agree.

---

## Controversial Strategies (Community Guidelines)

The following strategies are **not banned** but are discouraged when taken to extremes, as they reduce interactive, social play:

- Mass land destruction
- Heavy counterspell packages
- Extensive hand disruption
- Life-setting effects
- Infinite combos / "Spike" strategies in casual groups

The Commander Philosophy: prioritize **collective fun** over maximizing wins.

---

## Commander Brackets (Power Level Guide)

Wizards of the Coast introduced an official bracket system to help players communicate deck power level:

| Bracket | Description |
|---|---|
| **1 – Exhibition** | Very casual; preconstructed-level decks |
| **2 – Core** | Upgraded precons; budget builds |
| **3 – Advanced** | Tuned decks with synergistic strategies |
| **4 – Competitive (cEDH)** | Highly optimized; fastest possible win |

Discuss with your playgroup which bracket best fits your decks before playing.
