# The Marksman

**Role:** Assassin  
**Badge:** AK  
**Sigil:** ◎

## Victory
The King is eliminated.

## Announce
Creatures lose hexproof and shroud until end of turn. Deal damage to up to three targets equal to the highest converted mana cost among permanents you control. Activate only during your turn.

---
*"Any target within 100 meters. Work yourself within killing distance before taking the shot."*
