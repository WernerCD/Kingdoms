# The King

**Role:** King  
**Badge:** AK  
**Sigil:** ♔  

## Setup
Keep this card face up at all times. You are the starting player and begin with +10 life .

## Victory
All Assassins, Bandits and/or Renegades are eliminated.

## Warning
If you eliminate a player with a Knight role card: discard your hand, sacrifice half your permanents (rounded up), and lose half your life (rounded up). If you would win this turn, you lose instead.
