# Knight in Shining Armor

**Role:** Knight  
**Badge:** AK  
**Sigil:** ◈

## Victory
The King wins.

## Defeat
The King is eliminated.

## Announce
Whenever the King would be dealt damage by an opponent this turn, deal that much damage to that opponent instead. That opponent skips their next combat step. Activate only during combat after blockers are declared.
