# The Mimic

**Role:** Renegade  
**Badge:** AK  
**Sigil:** ◑

## Victory
Your teammate wins.

## Defeat
You or a teammate are eliminated.

## Announce
When you have 20 or less life, Announce immediately — draw a card.

## Special
**Passive:** At the beginning of your upkeep, target player loses 1 life. Another target player gains 1 life. Whenever you and any opponent have the same life total, that opponent is eliminated.

---
*"You are immortal — but these frail bodies decay. To survive, you must find other lives to devour."*
