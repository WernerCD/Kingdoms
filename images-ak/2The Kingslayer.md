# The Kingslayer

**Role:** Knight  
**Badge:** AK  
**Sigil:** ⛓  

## Victory
The King wins.

## Defeat
The King is eliminated.

## Announce
When you eliminate the King, Announce immediately — the King chooses another player (not you). That player gains the King role card. You have a replacement Defeat condition. The King cannot choose you as the new King.

*"Protect your King. Turning against him is a last resort — when no other path survives."*
