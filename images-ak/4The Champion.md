# The Champion

**Role:** Renegade  
**Badge:** AK  
**Sigil:** ◈  

## Victory
You personally eliminate the player you Challenged.

## Announce
Declare target player your Challenger . Until the start of your next turn, no other player may target the Challenger with spells or abilities. The Challenger must attack you if able. Activate only during your turn.

## Defeat
You are eliminated, or the Challenger is eliminated by another player.
