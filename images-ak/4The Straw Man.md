# The Straw Man

**Role:** Renegade  
**Badge:** AK  
**Sigil:** ☽  

## Victory
You are the first person eliminated and another player eliminated you.

## Announce
Until your next turn, all opponents' creatures must attack you if able. Activate only on your turn.

## Next Upkeep
At the beginning of your next upkeep, if you're still alive, your win condition is replaced — you win by eliminating yourself. (Loss of life, empty library, commander damage, or poison.)
