# The Bear Trainer

**Role:** Assassin  
**Badge:** AK  
**Sigil:** 🐻

## Victory
The King is eliminated.

## Announce
Create three 2/2 green Bear creature tokens named War Bear. They gain haste and have " {T}: This creature deals damage equal to its power to target creature. If a creature dealt damage this way dies this turn, put a +1/+1 counter on this creature." Activate only during your turn, and only if an opponent has 30 or less life.
