# The Cultist

**Role:** Renegade  
**Badge:** AK  
**Sigil:** ✶  

## Victory
More than half of the players in the game are Cultists.

## Announce
Whenever you eliminate an opponent, Announce immediately — that player's role card becomes a copy of the Cultist (retains King type if they were the King). Remove all commander damage and poison counters from that player. Their life total becomes 10.

---
*"Convert the fallen. Once more than half are yours — the kingdom belongs to the cult."*
