# The Sellsword

**Role:** Renegade  
**Badge:** AK  
**Sigil:** ⚔  

## Victory
Your teammate wins.

## Defeat
You or your teammate are eliminated.

## Announce
When a player Announces, Announce immediately — starting with that player, each bids any amount of life in turn order. The highest bidder loses life equal to their bid and sacrifices a quarter of their lands (rounded up). That player becomes your teammate . Note: if your teammate's role is face down, you won't know your new win condition — follow their lead.

---
*"Fight for the highest bidder. See who can offer the largest payment and join their cause."*
