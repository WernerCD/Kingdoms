# The Necromancer

**Role:** Bandit  
**Badge:** AK  
**Sigil:** ☠

## Victory
All non-teammates are eliminated.

## Defeat
You or a teammate are eliminated.

## Announce
Exile all creature cards from target player's graveyard. An opponent with a face-up role card separates those cards into two piles. Put all cards from one pile onto the battlefield under your control and exile the rest. Activate only during your turn.
