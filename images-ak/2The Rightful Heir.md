# The Rightful Heir

**Role:** Knight  
**Badge:** AK  
**Sigil:** ♔

## Victory
The King wins. If you have ascended to the throne, you win under the King's victory condition.

## Defeat
The King is eliminated while you are not face-up.

## Announce
When you eliminate any player, Announce immediately. While face-up: if the King is eliminated, you gain the King role card and gain 10 life. The game continues under your reign.

---
*"Prove yourself. Eliminate an enemy. Then — only then — take the throne."*
