# The Zealot

**Role:** Bandit  
**Badge:** AK  
**Sigil:** ⚑  

## Victory
All non-teammates are eliminated.

## Defeat
You or a teammate are eliminated.

## Announce
Each player with a face-up role card reveals cards from the top of their library until they reveal a creature card. You may put each creature revealed this way onto the battlefield under your control. Shuffle all other revealed cards into their owners' libraries. Those creatures gain vigilance, haste, and double strike until end of turn. Activate only during your turn.
