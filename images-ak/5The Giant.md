# The Giant

**Role:** Bandit  
**Badge:** AK  
**Sigil:** 👊  

## Victory
All non-Bandits are eliminated.

## Defeat
You or a teammate are eliminated.

## Announce
The creature with the highest combined power and toughness is unaffected. Destroy all other creatures. (In a tie, the active player chooses.) Activate only during your turn.
