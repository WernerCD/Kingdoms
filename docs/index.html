Quick Reference · Advanced Kingdoms

Player Count

5p: 1 King · 1 Knight · 2 Assassins · 1 Renegade
6p: 1 King · 1 Knight · 2 Bandits · 2 Assassins
7p: 1 King · 1 Knight · 2 Bandits · 2 Assassins · 1 Renegade

Announce

Turn your face-down role card face up to activate your role's special ability. Face-up roles cannot Announce again. All role abilities use the stack but cannot be countered, targeted, or exiled.

Winning

Only role-card Victory conditions can win the game. MTG "win the game" effects have no effect. First player to meet their Victory condition wins; simultaneous conditions share the win.

Elimination

You eliminate a player if an effect you control causes them to lose the game. When eliminated, reveal your role card if face-down. Knights eliminating the King trigger severe penalties.

Card Versions

Cards marked AK are official Advanced Kingdoms originals. Cards marked EK are originals from Expanded Kingdoms. Cards marked Variant are house additions. Mix and match freely from each role's pool when dealing.

King Setup

The King reveals their card immediately, takes the first turn, and begins with +10 life. Only the King role is public at game start. Bandits (if used) identify each other with eyes-closed reveal before play begins.
