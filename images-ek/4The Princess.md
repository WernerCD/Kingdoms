# The Princess

**Role:** Noble  
**Badge:** EK  
**Sigil:** ♛

## Victory
When all players are Nobles or their teammates, you all win the game.

## Announce
(Trigger) Whenever a creature attacks you, you may turn The Princess face up. If you do, prevent all combat damage dealt to you this turn. Creatures your opponents control attack the player whose creature attacked you, if able, until that player's next turn.
