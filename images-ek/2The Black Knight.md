# The Black Knight

**Role:** Knight  
**Badge:** EK  
**Sigil:** ☽  

## Victory
The King wins the game. When there is no King, you lose the game if face down.

## Announce
(Trigger) If you would lose the game for any reason other than The King dying, instead turn face up. Remove all commander damage and poison counters on you. Your life total becomes 10.

## Special
If face up and no other players remain, you win the game.
