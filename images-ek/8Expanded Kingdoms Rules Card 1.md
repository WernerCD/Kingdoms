# Expanded Kingdoms Rules Card 1

**Role:** Rules  
**Badge:** EK  

## Role Reveal
Cards begin face-down. Flip face-up to use your Announce ability (once per game).
Some abilities trigger automatically — flip face-up immediately when triggered.
The King is always face-up from the start.

## Victory
The game ends when any role's Victory condition is met. Reveal all role cards at game end.
Multiple roles may win simultaneously (e.g., King and all Knights win together).
