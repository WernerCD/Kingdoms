# The Imposter

**Role:** Renegade  
**Badge:** EK  
**Sigil:** 🎭

## Announce
**Accuse:** Once per game: Name a face-down player as "The Jester." That player must immediately reveal their role card. If they are the Jester, this counts as you causing the Jester to be revealed and triggers The Imposter's Special.

## Special
Whenever you cause an opponent to lose the game, or if you cause a Jester to be revealed, you may reveal The Imposter. If you do, that player gives you their role card face down. You may keep it in addition to The Imposter — if you do, put all other role cards you control into the unused pile.
