# The Slave

**Role:** Token  
**Badge:** EK  
**Sigil:** ⛓  

## Special
Created by The Slaver's Special ability when you would eliminate a non-King player.
You join The Slaver in a two-headed giant team, sharing their life total and turn.
All commander damage and poison counters are removed from you.

## Victory
When no player is King, reveal The Slaver — all Bandits and Slaves win the game.
