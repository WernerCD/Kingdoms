# The Bastard

**Role:** Knight  
**Badge:** EK  
**Sigil:** ◇  

## Victory
When the only other players are the King, their teammates and your teammates, reveal The Bastard — you all win the game together. When there is no King, you lose the game if face down.

## Announce
(Trigger) When you cause a non-King player to lose the game, you may turn The Bastard face up.

## Special
When face up and there is no King, The Bastard becomes a copy of The King.
