# The Pirate

**Role:** Bandit  
**Badge:** EK  
**Sigil:** ⚓  

## Victory
When no player is King, reveal The Pirate — all Bandits win the game.

## Announce
Gain control of target permanent until end of turn. Untap it. It gains haste until end of turn.
