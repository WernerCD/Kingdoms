# The Illusionist

**Role:** Noble  
**Badge:** EK  
**Sigil:** ◐  

## Victory
When all players are Nobles or their teammates, you all win the game.

## Announce
Choose an activated ability of a face up role card. You may activate that ability without paying its cost. When you cause a player to lose the game, you may turn any number of Noble role cards face down.
