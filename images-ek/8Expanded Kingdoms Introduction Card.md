# Expanded Kingdoms Introduction Card

**Role:** Rules  
**Badge:** EK  

## Introduction
Welcome to Expanded Kingdoms — a role-based variant for Commander (EDH).
Each player is secretly assigned a role card that determines their win condition.
Roles are kept face-down until triggered or revealed.

## Setup
Choose one player to be the King. Give them The King card (kept face-up).
Shuffle the remaining role cards and deal one face-down to each other player.
Bandits secretly identify each other at game start.
