# The Burglar

**Role:** Bandit  
**Badge:** EK  
**Sigil:** ⚿  

## Victory
When no player is King, reveal The Burglar — all Bandits win the game.

## Announce
Gain hexproof until end of turn. Then gain control of all lands target opponent controls until end of turn. Untap them.
