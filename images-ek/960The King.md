# The King (960px)

**Role:** King  
**Badge:** EK  
**Sigil:** ♔

## Victory
All Assassins, Bandits and/or Renegades are eliminated.

## Special
**Setup:** Keep this card face up at all times. You are the starting player and begin with +10 life .

{T}, exile a card from your hand (or pay {4} ): Choose one — Another player of your choice adds {C}{C} to their mana pool Target player draws two cards You gain 5 life Activate only during your main phase.

**Flip Reference:** King — Face up at game start. Public role. Bandits — Secret peek at game start: all close eyes, Bandits open eyes to identify each other, then close eyes. Cards stay face down. All others — Start face down. Flip face up to Announce your special ability (once per game).
