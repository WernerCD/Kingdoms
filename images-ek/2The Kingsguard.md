# The Kingsguard

**Role:** Knight  
**Badge:** EK  
**Sigil:** 🛡

## Victory
When the only other players are the King, their teammates and your teammates, reveal The Kingsguard — you all win the game. When there is no King, you lose the game.

## Announce
(Trigger) If combat damage from unblocked attacking creatures could cause a King to lose the game, you may turn The Kingsguard face up. If you do, prevent all combat damage that would be dealt this turn.
