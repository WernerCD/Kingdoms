# The Dragonkeeper

**Role:** Bandit  
**Badge:** EK  
**Sigil:** 🐉  

## Victory
When no player is King, reveal The Dragonkeeper — all Bandits win the game.

## Announce
Put three 1/1 red Dragon tokens with flying and haste onto the battlefield. They have "{T}: This creature deals damage equal to its power to target creature. If it dies this turn from this damage, put a +1/+1 counter on this creature."
