# The Jester (960px)

**Role:** Renegade  
**Badge:** EK  
**Sigil:** 🃏

## Victory
Win if you are eliminated by the King — through combat damage or a spell they control — while the King is still alive.

## Special
You may reveal your role at any time. If you do, the King must attack you on their next available attack step.
