# The Gunslinger

**Role:** Bandit  
**Badge:** EK  
**Sigil:** 🔫  

## Victory
When no player is King, reveal The Gunslinger — all Bandits win the game.

## Announce
Deal 3 damage to target player or creature. If this damage would be lethal, that player or that creature's controller loses 5 life instead.
