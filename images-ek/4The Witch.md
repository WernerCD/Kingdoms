# The Witch

**Role:** Renegade  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ☽  

## Victory
Win if both the King and all Knights are eliminated. You may win even if you yourself are also eliminated.

## Announce
(Trigger) At the beginning of any player's upkeep, you may turn The Witch face up. If you do, place a curse on target player — at the beginning of each of that player's upkeeps, they lose 2 life. This curse persists until that player is eliminated.

## Special
Once per game: Look at target player's hand. You may not reveal what you saw.
