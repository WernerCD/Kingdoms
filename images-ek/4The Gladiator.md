# The Gladiator

**Role:** Renegade  
**Badge:** EK  
**Sigil:** ⚔  

## Victory
You personally eliminate 3 or more opponents during the game. Reveal The Gladiator — you win regardless of any other win condition.

## Announce
Challenge target opponent to combat — their creatures must attack you next turn, and your creatures must attack them. Neither player may block with creatures not involved in this challenge.
