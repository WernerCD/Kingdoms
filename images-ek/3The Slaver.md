# The Slaver

**Role:** Bandit  
**Badge:** EK  
**Sigil:** ⛓  

## Victory
When no player is King, reveal The Slaver — all Bandits and Slaves win the game.

## Special
(Face down) If face down and you would cause a non-King player to lose, instead flip face up and that player gains The Slave role card. Remove all commander damage and poison counters from that player. That player joins you in a two-headed giant team sharing your life total and turn.
