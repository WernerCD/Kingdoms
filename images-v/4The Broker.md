# The Broker

**Role:** Renegade  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ✒  

## Victory
At game end you are a **teammate of a winning player**.

## Announce
**Once per game,** at instant speed: choose a player who has no teammates. You become their teammate.

## Defeat
You are eliminated, or the game ends with you having no teammate.
