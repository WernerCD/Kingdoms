# The Lone Wolf

**Role:** Assassin  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ◑  

## Victory
The King is eliminated AND **you are the only surviving Assassin.** Other living Assassins at game end deny your victory.

## Special
You win alone. Your fellow Assassins are obstacles, not allies. You do not share a win condition with them.
