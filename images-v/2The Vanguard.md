# The Vanguard

**Role:** Knight  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ⚔  

## Victory
The King wins.

## Defeat
The King is eliminated.

## Special
Once per game: During your declare attackers step, your creatures may attack any player regardless of effects that would restrict your attack targets this turn. Creatures you control gain +1/+0 and trample until end of turn.
