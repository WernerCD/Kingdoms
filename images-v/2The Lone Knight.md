# The Lone Knight

**Role:** Knight  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ◎  

## Victory
The King wins **AND** you are still alive at the moment of victory. You do not share this win with other Knights who were eliminated before the game ended.

## Defeat
The King is eliminated, or the King wins but you are already eliminated.
