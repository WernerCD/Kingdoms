# The Cursed Crown

**Role:** King  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ⊖  

## Victory
All Assassins, Bandits and/or Renegades are eliminated.

## Special
**Setup:** Keep face up. Starting player. Begin with **+20 life** instead of the standard +10.
**Curse:** At the beginning of each of your upkeeps, **lose 1 life for each surviving enemy** (Assassin, Bandit or Renegade). This loss cannot be prevented or replaced.

---
*"The crown gives more than it takes — until it doesn't."*
