# The Last Bandit

**Role:** Bandit  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ◑  

## Setup
At most **one solo-win card per faction** may be dealt in a game. If a second would be dealt, shuffle it back and redraw.

## Victory
All non-teammates are eliminated AND **you are the only surviving Bandit.** Fellow Bandits count as teammates for the first clause — you must still outlast them. Other living Bandits at game end deny your victory.

## Defeat
You are eliminated, or the game ends with another Bandit still alive.
