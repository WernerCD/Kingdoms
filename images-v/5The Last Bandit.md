# The Last Bandit

**Role:** Bandit  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ◑  

## Victory
All non-Bandits are eliminated AND **you are the only surviving Bandit.** Other living Bandits at game end deny your victory.

## Defeat
You are eliminated, or the game ends with another Bandit still alive.

*"When the dust settles, only one of us gets to walk out."*
