# The Avenger

**Role:** Knight  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ⚜  

## Victory
The King wins. **OR:** you personally deal the killing blow to the player who eliminated the King.

## Announce
When the King is eliminated, **Announce immediately** — the game does not end. **This replaces your standard Knight Defeat condition, and Assassin Victory does not resolve until the end of your next turn.** You have until the end of your next turn to personally deal the killing blow to the player who eliminated the King. If you succeed, you win. If you fail or are eliminated first, you lose.

## Defeat
The King is eliminated and you do not eliminate their killer before the end of your next turn. Or you are eliminated first.
