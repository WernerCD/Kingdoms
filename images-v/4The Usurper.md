# The Usurper

**Role:** Renegade  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ♛  

## Victory
When you personally deal the final damage to eliminate the King, reveal this card. You **gain the King role card** and gain 10 life. The game continues under your reign, and you now win under the King's Victory condition.
