# The Bloodthirsty King

**Role:** King  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ⊕  

## Setup
Keep face up. Starting player. Begin with **+10 life.**

## Victory
All Assassins, Bandits and/or Renegades are eliminated, **AND** you personally dealt the final damage to eliminate at least one opponent.

## Warning
Standard Knight-elimination penalty applies. If all enemies are gone but you have no personal kill, you do not win immediately — instead you win at the **beginning of your next end step**, giving the table one turn to stop you.
