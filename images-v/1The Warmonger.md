# The Warmonger

**Role:** King  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ⚒  

## Setup
Keep face up. Starting player. Begin with **+10 life.**

## Victory
All Assassins, Bandits and/or Renegades are eliminated.

## Aggression
You **must attack** during each of your combat phases if able. If your combat phase ends without you having attacked, lose 5 life.

---
*"A King who does not fight is not a King — he is a target."*
