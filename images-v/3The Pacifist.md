# The Pacifist

**Role:** Assassin  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ✦  

## Victory
The King is eliminated and at least one Assassin survives. **However,** you must never have dealt damage to the King with a spell or ability you control that **targets** them, or with your **attacking creatures**.

## Special
You must manipulate other players into killing the King on your behalf. If you ever damage the King that way, your Victory condition is permanently voided this game. **Incidental, redirected or symmetrical damage does not void it.**
