# The Pacifist

**Role:** Assassin  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ✦  

## Victory
The King is eliminated and at least one Assassin survives. **However,** you must never have personally dealt damage to the King — not combat, not spells, not abilities.

## Special
You must manipulate other players into killing the King on your behalf. If you ever damage the King directly, your Victory condition is permanently voided this game.
