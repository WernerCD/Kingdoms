# The Plunderer

**Role:** Bandit  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ◇  

## Victory
All non-Bandits are eliminated.

## Defeat
You or a teammate are eliminated.

## Special
**Once per game:** If you would be eliminated, you may instead survive at 1 life. This ability may only trigger once and cannot be copied.
