# Kill Jack

**Role:** Renegade  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** 🎯  

## Victory
The player known at this table as **"Jack"** is eliminated.

## If You Are Jack
Your win condition changes: **Survive to the end of the game.** You may reveal this card at game start. If you do, all other players must verbally acknowledge, in turn order, that Jack deserved this.

## Reveal
Turn this card face up at any time. The player named Jack loses 10 life and creatures they control lose all abilities until end of turn. If Jack is eliminated while this card is face up, you win immediately regardless of any other win condition.

## No Jack Present
Before the game begins, secretly choose any player at the table. That player is your **"Jack"** for all purposes of this card.
