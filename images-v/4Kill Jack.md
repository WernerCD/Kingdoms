# Kill Jack

**Role:** Renegade  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** 🎯  

## Setup
At game start the table designates one player as **Jack**. If no player is so named, Jack is the player to the King's left.

## Victory
The player known at this table as **"Jack"** is eliminated.

## Reveal
Turn this card face up at any time. The player named Jack loses 10 life and creatures they control lose all abilities until end of turn. If Jack is eliminated while this card is face up, you win immediately regardless of any other win condition.
