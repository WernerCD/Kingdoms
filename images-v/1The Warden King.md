# The Warden King

**Role:** King  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ⚖  

## Setup
Keep this card **face up** at all times. You are the starting player and begin with **+10 life**.

## Victory
All Assassins, Bandits and/or Renegades are eliminated.

## Warden's Judgment
**{T}**, pay **3 life**: Choose one —
- Tap all creatures target opponent controls; they don't untap during their next untap step
- Exile a card from any player's graveyard
- Until your next upkeep, all damage that would be dealt to you is instead dealt to its source's controller
*Activate only during your main phase.*
