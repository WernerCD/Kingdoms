# The High King

**Role:** King  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ♛  

## Victory
All Assassins, Bandits and/or Renegades are eliminated.

## Announce
**Royal Decree:** **{T}**, pay **{2}**: Choose one —
- Target Knight draws a card and gains 3 life
- Choose a player — they cannot attack you until your next main phase
- Secretly look at any one player's hidden role card
*Activate only during your main phase.*

## Special
**Setup:** Keep this card **face up** at all times. You are the starting player and begin with **+15 life**.
