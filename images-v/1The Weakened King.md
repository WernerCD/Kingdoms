# The Weakened King

**Role:** King  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ◐  

## Victory
All Assassins, Bandits and/or Renegades are eliminated.

## Special
**Setup:** Keep face up. Starting player. Begin with **40 life** instead of the King's standard 50.
**Passive:** Whenever any player is eliminated from the game, **gain 10 life.** The King grows stronger as the field narrows.

---
*"Underestimated at first. Undefeatable at the end."*
