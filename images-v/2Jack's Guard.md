# Jack's Guard

**Role:** Knight  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** 🛡  

## Victory
The player known at this table as **"Jack"** wins — regardless of Jack's actual role.

## Defeat
Jack is eliminated. *(Unless you are Jack — see below.)*

## Announce
**Reveal:** When Jack is targeted by a spell or ability an opponent controls, or would receive combat damage, you may reveal this card. If you do, you become the target or receive that damage instead. If this eliminates you, Jack gains 10 life. You may also reveal voluntarily at any time; Jack gains 5 life.

## Special
If You're Jack: Your Victory would be circular, so it changes: **survive to the end of the game.** Your Reveal cannot intercept on your own behalf — instead you may reveal at any time to gain **10 life** and draw a card.
If No Jack: Before the game begins, secretly write down any player — they are your **"Jack"** for all purposes of this card, and only you know it. You may reveal this card at any time to **name them aloud**: they gain **10 life**, draw a card, and until the start of your next turn all damage that would be dealt to them is dealt to its **source's controller** instead. If you cannot produce your note, you lose.
