# Jack's Guard

**Role:** Knight  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** 🛡  

## Setup
At game start the table designates one player as **Jack**. If no player is so named, Jack is the player to the King's left.

## Victory
The player known at this table as **"Jack"** wins — regardless of Jack's actual role.

## Defeat
Jack is eliminated.

## Reveal
When Jack is targeted by a spell or ability an opponent controls, or would receive combat damage, you may reveal this card. If you do, you become the target or receive that damage instead. If this eliminates you, Jack gains 10 life. You may also reveal voluntarily at any time; Jack gains 5 life.
