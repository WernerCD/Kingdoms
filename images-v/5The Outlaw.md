# The Outlaw

**Role:** Bandit  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ⊗  

## Victory
All non-teammates are eliminated.

## Defeat
You or a teammate are eliminated.

## Special
Once per game: Choose target player. That player reveals their hand to you. You may choose one card revealed this way — that player discards it.
