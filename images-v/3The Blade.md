# The Blade

**Role:** Assassin  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ⚔  

## Victory
The King is eliminated. If **you** personally dealt the killing blow, you win alone — other Assassins do not share your victory.

## Special
**Setup:** At most **one solo-win card per faction** may be dealt in a game. If a second would be dealt, shuffle it back and redraw.
Track whether you dealt the final damage to eliminate the King. If so, the solo-win condition applies.
