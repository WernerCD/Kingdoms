# The Cutthroat

**Role:** Bandit  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ☠  

## Victory
All non-Bandits are eliminated.

## Defeat
You or a teammate are eliminated.

## Special
Once per game: During your declare attackers step, your creatures may attack any player regardless of effects that would restrict your attack targets this turn. Each creature you control that deals combat damage to a player this turn draws you a card.
