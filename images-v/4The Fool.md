# The Fool

**Role:** Renegade  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** 🎪  

## Victory
Win if you are **eliminated by the King** — through combat damage or a spell they control — while the King is still alive. If this happens, the **King loses the game as well.**

## Announce
You may turn **The Fool** face up at any time. If you do, the King must **attack you** on each of their attack steps until you are eliminated, unless the King **loses 3 life** to decline that attack. Other players may not block or redirect the King's attacks that target you.

## Special
When you are eliminated by the King while this card is face up, the King must **reveal their hand** to all players before The Fool's Victory is resolved.
