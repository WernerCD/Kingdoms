# The Bounty Hunter

**Role:** Assassin  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ⊗  

## Setup
At game start, **secretly write down the name of one non-King player.** Fold it face-down beside your role card. You may name a teammate — this is intentional.

## Victory
The King is eliminated **AND** your named target is also eliminated before the game ends. You must survive to claim this win.
