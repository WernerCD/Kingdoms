# The Infiltrator

**Role:** Assassin  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** 🎭  

## Victory
The King is eliminated and at least one Assassin survives to the end.

## Announce
When you reveal yourself, force target player to reveal their role card to all players. Draw 2 cards. *Activate only during your turn.*

## Special
The first time each turn a player **publicly names you as an Assassin**, that player loses 2 life. Only you may reveal your own role.
