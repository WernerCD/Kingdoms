# The Arsonist

**Role:** Bandit  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** 🔥  

## Victory
All non-teammates are eliminated.
**Bonus Victory:** **Alternate win:** If you control **15 or more non-land permanents** when **another player's** Victory condition is met, you win instead — even if your team does not.

## Defeat
You or a teammate are eliminated.

## Special
**Setup:** At most **one solo-win card per faction** may be dealt in a game. If a second would be dealt, shuffle it back and redraw.
