# The Poisoner

**Role:** Assassin  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ⚗  

## Victory
The King is eliminated and at least one Assassin survives to the end.

## Special
**Once per game:** Mark a target player as Poisoned. They lose 2 life at the start of each of their upkeeps. This is a role-card effect: per **Rule 5** it cannot be targeted, countered, or exiled. It ends only when you are eliminated.
