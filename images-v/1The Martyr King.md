# The Martyr King

**Role:** King  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ✝  

## Setup
Keep face up. Starting player. Begin with **+10 life.**

## Victory
All Assassins, Bandits and/or Renegades are eliminated — **even if you were eliminated earlier.**

## Special
If the King is eliminated, the game does not end. **While this card is in play, each Knight's Defeat condition is replaced with "You are eliminated."** Knights retain their win condition; if all surviving Knights subsequently win, the King wins posthumously alongside them. **Assassin Victory additionally requires that all Knights be eliminated.**
