# The Quartermaster

**Role:** Bandit  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ⚙  

## Victory
All non-teammates are eliminated.

## Defeat
**You** are eliminated. *(Unlike other Bandits, a teammate's elimination does not defeat you.)*

## Special
**Once per game:** When a teammate would be eliminated, they instead survive at **1 life**.

---
*"I count the bodies. I don't add to them."*
