# The Turncoat

**Role:** Knight  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ⇄  

## Victory
The King wins. **OR:** if you have defected, the King is eliminated and you are alive at game end.

## Defeat
The King is eliminated while this card is still face down.

## Announce
Once, at the beginning of any upkeep, reveal this card to **defect**. You cease to be a teammate of the King and Knights, and become a teammate of the Assassins for the rest of the game.
