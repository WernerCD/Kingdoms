# The Raider

**Role:** Bandit  
**Badge:** Variant  
**Sigil:** ⚑  

## Victory
All non-Bandits are eliminated.

## Defeat
You or a teammate are eliminated.

## Special
Whenever **any player is eliminated** from the game, draw a card. This triggers regardless of who caused the elimination.
